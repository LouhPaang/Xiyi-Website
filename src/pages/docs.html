Approach

Learn the patterns, then make them yours.

Xiyi is best approached in small, regular sessions. Notice a sound. Try a word aloud. Return to an idea until it feels familiar. Let understanding arrive through use rather than memorisation alone.

Learning path

  1. 01

    Meet the sounds

    Start by listening for the character and rhythm of the language.

  2. 02

    Explore the structure

    See how words and relationships come together to carry thought.

  3. 03

    Use what you know

    Read, write, and share small expressions of your own.

Open to conversation

Help the language grow.

Xiyi is developed in the open. Questions, observations, and contributions are welcome in the project repository.
